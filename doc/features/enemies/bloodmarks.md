# Blood marks

## Requirements
* Bullets that hit an enemy leave blood marks.
* Blood marks are circular surface marks.
* Larger-caliber ammunition leaves larger blood marks.
* An enemy retains at most 24 blood marks, replacing the oldest mark first.

## User scenarios
### 1. A player bullet creates a blood mark
### 2. A blood mark uses a circular surface patch
### 3. A 12-gauge slug creates a larger mark than a 9 mm bullet
### 4. The oldest blood mark is replaced when the limit is exceeded
