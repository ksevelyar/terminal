# Hanging lamps

## Requirements
* Hanging lamps are still when they spawn.
* Shooting a lamp makes it swing visibly.
* A lamp swings like a pendulum whose period reflects its cable length.
* A lamp's swing loses amplitude over time.
* A shot lamp completes at least five swings within 15 seconds and settles within 50 seconds.

## User scenarios
### 1. A lamp hangs still when it spawns
### 2. A shot lamp completes five swings within fifteen seconds
### 3. A lamp's swing period matches its cable length
### 4. A lamp's swing amplitude decays monotonically
### 5. A lamp's swing amplitude reflects bullet momentum
### 6. A shot lamp settles within the documented time
