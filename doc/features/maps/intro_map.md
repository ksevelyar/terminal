# Intro map

## Requirements
* Every underground entrance aligns with its matching tower level.
* Each underground passage has a 6 m clear height and a width matching its tower opening.
* Underground passages stay outside the tower shell and preserve the surfaces beside each opening.
* Passage floors continuously connect tower landings and corridors.
* Sandfield terrain is finite and changes smoothly across the playable area.
* The intro map starts unpaused with the player above the terrain.

## User scenarios
### 1. Underground entrances align with every tower connector
### 2. Every underground passage matches its beveled edge's width and height
### 3. Underground corridor colliders stay outside their beveled tower faces
### 4. An underground entrance keeps both adjacent straight tower faces intact
### 5. An underground passage floor continuously connects its landing and corridor
### 6. Sandfield terrain remains finite and smooth at two-meter intervals
### 7. The intro map starts with a sandfield and player above the terrain
