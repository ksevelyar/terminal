# Path finding

## Requirements
* Players cannot walk through solid obstacles.
* Players can walk up and over stairs.
* Fighters navigate around obstacles to reach players.
* Fighters can navigate over stairs to reach players.

## User scenarios
### 1. A player pressing W is blocked by a pillar
### 2. A player pressing W is stopped by a pillar's south face
### 3. A player walks over a staircase
### 4. A fighter walks over a staircase to reach the player
### 5. A fighter walks around a pillar to reach the player
