# Muzzle flash

## Requirements
* Firing the player's weapon briefly displays a muzzle flash.
* A muzzle flash provides a brief light effect.
* Gunner firearms have muzzle flashes.
* Fighter melee weapons do not have muzzle flashes.

## User scenarios
### 1. Firing the player's weapon shows and hides its muzzle flash
### 2. A gunner weapon has a muzzle flash and a fighter weapon does not
