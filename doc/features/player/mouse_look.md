# Mouse look

## Requirements
* Starting a game creates a player camera.
* Mouse movement controls player yaw and pitch.
* Player pitch is limited to prevent overturning.
* Keyboard movement moves the player forward in their facing direction.
* Ground movement does not change the player's height.

## User scenarios
### 1. Starting the game spawns the player camera
### 2. Moving the mouse rotates player yaw
### 3. Moving the mouse rotates player pitch
### 4. Player pitch is clamped
### 5. Pressing W moves the player forward
### 6. Player movement stays on the horizontal plane
